**Overview**

The Pakistani Creative Collective (PCC) is a curated, searchable database of Pakistani creatives working in film, music, and media. It is built and managed by Aneesa Talks LLC. The core purpose is to make Pakistani creative talent findable, hirable, and connected across the globe while keeping individual contact information private. All inquiries route through Aneesa Talks.

The platform is currently in pre-launch. Registration is invite and referral only. Public launch is planned for Fall 2026\.

For reference, the current public-facing PCC page is here: [aneesatalks.com/pakistani-creative-collective](http://aneesatalks.com/pakistani-creative-collective)  

The current enrollment form is here: [jotform.com/form/253118193860054](http://jotform.com/form/253118193860054)

**The Two User Types**

**1\. Unpaid Users:** Anyone who visits the platform without a paid account. They can see each creative's name, pronouns, bio (300-450 words), and one website or social media link of the creative's choosing. They cannot search or filter, view full profiles, or submit contact requests. Each month, a small selection of featured creatives will have their full profiles made public temporarily (see Monthly Featured Creatives below).

**2\. Paid Users:** Anyone who pays for an annual subscription, including creatives listed in the database. Paid users can see full creative profiles (with headshots), search and filter the database, and submit contact requests through Aneesa Talks. Being listed in the database is free; accessing the database as a user requires a paid subscription.

---

**Registration (Creatives Joining the Database)**

Registration is not publicly advertised. The enrollment form link is shared directly by Aneesa Talks via a personal invite. Recipients can forward the link to others they recommend, and those people can forward it further. Access to the form is link-based — anyone with the link can submit an application. No invite code or gated landing page is required to access the form itself.

Once submitted, Aneesa Talks reviews each application manually and approves or rejects at their discretion. Approved creatives are added to the database at no cost. Accessing the database as a paid user is a separate step and requires an annual subscription.

The enrollment form currently lives on Jotform and takes approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.

**User Experience Flows**

**Unpaid User**

1. User lands on the PCC database page.  
2. They can see each creative's name, pronouns, bio (300-450 words), and one website or social media link chosen by the creative.  
3. All other profile information is locked behind the paid tier.  
4. They are prompted to purchase an annual subscription to see full profiles, search and filter the database, and submit contact requests.  
5. Each month, a small selection of featured creative profiles are made fully public, giving unpaid users a preview of the paid tier and showcasing creatives who are actively available for work or collaboration.

**Paid User**

1. User purchases an annual subscription and logs in.  
2. They can search and filter the full database by role, experience level, medium, availability, language, location/willingness to travel, preferred project types, and rate range (if the creative has opted to share it).  
3. They view a creative's full profile.  
4. They submit a contact request form: who they are, what the project is, and what they're looking for.  
5. The request routes to Aneesa Talks, who reviews it and forwards it to the relevant creative.  
6. The creative decides whether they want to connect.  
7. If yes, the creative either reaches out to the requester directly via email or obtains Aneesa Talks' consent to share their email with the requester. Aneesa Talks, then hands off communications.  
8. If no or unavailable: Aneesa Talks relays this to the requester and either hands off or suggests alternative creatives from the database.  
9. For a more personalized matching or production consultation, the requester can hire Aneesa Talks directly.

**Creative Registration Flow (Current)**

1. Creative receives a personal invite from Aneesa Talks or a referral link forwarded by someone who was invited.  
2. They complete the Jotform enrollment form (approximately 10-15 minutes).  
3. Aneesa Talks reviews the submission manually.  
4. If approved, the creative is added to the database at no cost.  
5. Creative receives a confirmation email.

**Creative Registration Flow (Future Platform)**

1. Creative receives or is forwarded the enrollment form link.  
2. They complete the enrollment form on the platform (replacing the current Jotform).  
3. Submission triggers a notification to the admin (Aneesa Talks).  
4. Admin approves or rejects.  
5. If approved, a profile is auto-generated from their form data and goes live in the database.  
6. Creative receives a confirmation email with a link to view their profile.  
7. To access the full database as a paid user, the creative purchases an annual subscription separately.

**Payment Model**

The platform operates on an annual subscription model. Being listed in the database as a creative is free. Accessing the full database requires a paid annual subscription and applies to all users, including listed creatives.

**Pricing:**

* Early access (pre-launch invite registrants, first year only): $30/year  
* Standard (post-public launch, and all renewals): $50/year

The early access rate of $30/year applies to anyone who registers through the invite and referral system before the public launch in Fall 2026, for their first subscription year only. All renewals, including those of early registrants, revert to the standard rate of $50/year. After public launch, all new subscribers pay $50/year from the start.

For more personalized matching or production support beyond the platform, users can hire Aneesa Talks to consult or produce. This is a separate service offering.

**What Is Public vs. Private**

**Publicly visible (unpaid users):**

* Name  
* Pronouns  
* Bio (300-450 words)  
* One website or social media link, chosen by the creative to best reflect their work

**Monthly featured profiles (publicly visible for that month only):**

* Everything in the paid tier view except information visible only to the admin  
* Featured creatives are selected by Aneesa Talks from those who have requested to be featured or who Aneesa Talks identifies as a good fit  
* This feature launches once the platform is stable post-launch

**Visible to paid users:**

* Professional headshot  
* Role(s)  
* Experience level  
* Medium(s)  
* Work samples (title, role, project link)  
* Notable achievements  
* Languages spoken  
* Availability status  
* Preferred project types  
* Previous collaborators (if provided)  
* Rate structure and rate range (if the creative has opted to share it)  
* All additional social and professional links  
* Ability to submit a contact request

**Private (visible to Aneesa Talks admin only):**

* Email address  
* Phone number  
* Home address  
* Union/guild memberships  
* Professional references  
* Education/training background  
* Equipment list  
* Collaboration preferences  
* What they're looking for from the PCC  
* Promo materials consent status  
* Rate information for creatives who have not opted to make it visible  
* Feature requests and accompanying notes

**Data We Are Currently Collecting (via Jotform)**

*Basic Information:* Name, pronouns, email, phone (optional), how they heard about the PCC, professional headshot link, address.

*Bio & Background:* Professional bio (300-450 words), previous collaborators, union/guild memberships, education/training background, what they're looking for from the PCC, additional notes.

*Professional Role(s):* Extensive checklist across Directing, Writing, Producing, Camera, Lighting & Grip, Sound, Editing & Post-Production, Music, Production Design/Art, Styling, Casting & Talent, Creative & Design, Marketing/Publicity/Media, Film Criticism & Festivals, and Events. Free-text field for roles not listed.

*Medium(s):* Checklist including Short Film, Feature Film, Documentary, Music Video, Animation, Photography, Theater, and more.

*Experience Level:* Self-assessed tier (Emerging / Developing / Established / Accomplished / Veteran), years of professional experience, number of completed projects, notable achievements.

*Portfolio:* Up to 3 work samples (title, medium, year, role, project link), website/portfolio URL, IMDb, Instagram, Vimeo/YouTube, LinkedIn. Creative selects which single link is displayed publicly.

*Work for Hire:* Rate structure (day/weekly/project/hourly/negotiable), standard rate range. Creative opts in to having this visible to paid users.

*Special Skills & Equipment:* Availability status, preferred project types, languages spoken, special skills or certifications, equipment ownership and list.

*Collaboration Preferences:* Experience level of collaborators they want to work with.

*Consent & Verification:* Six checkboxes covering data consent, privacy understanding, accuracy confirmation, verification consent, update obligation, and discretionary rejection clause. Referral name field.

**Note:** The enrollment form will need two additions: a field for the creative to select which link displays publicly, and a field for the creative to opt in to displaying their rate information to paid users.

**How data is currently collected:** Via Jotform linked in direct outreach emails. Recipients can forward the link to others they recommend. Submissions compile into a connected spreadsheet that Aneesa Talks reviews manually. Approval and onboarding are handled via direct email.

**Contact Request Flow (Detail)**

1. Paid user submits a contact request for a specific creative.  
2. Aneesa Talks receives the request and reviews it.  
3. Aneesa Talks forwards the request to the creative.  
4. Creative responds with one of the following:  
   * Yes, I want to connect — reaches out to the requester directly via email, or gives Aneesa Talks consent to share their email with the requester. Aneesa Talks hands off all further communications.  
   * No or unavailable — Aneesa Talks relays this to the requester and either hands off or suggests alternative creatives from the database.  
5. For a more personalized recommendation or production support, the requester can hire Aneesa Talks as a consultant or producer. This is a separate paid service.

**Monthly Featured Creatives**

Once the platform is stable post-launch, Aneesa Talks will select a small group of creatives each month whose full profiles (minus admin-only information) will be made temporarily public. Featured creatives will be those who are open to work for hire or actively seeking collaborators for a specific project.

**Feature Request Flow:**

1. A logged-in creative visits the PCC database page and clicks the "Request to Be Featured" button.  
2. A text box appears where the creative explains why they want to be featured and what kind of work or collaborators they are looking for.  
3. The request is submitted and routed to the admin (Aneesa Talks).  
4. Aneesa Talks reviews all feature requests alongside any creatives they identify independently and selects that month's featured group.  
5. Selected creatives are notified before their profile goes public.

This feature serves two purposes: it gives unpaid users a meaningful preview of the database and incentivizes upgrading to a paid subscription, and it gives featured creatives additional visibility beyond the paid user base.
